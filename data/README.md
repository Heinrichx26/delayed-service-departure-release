# Numerical inputs

The study uses public United States Bureau of Transportation Statistics
(BTS) On-Time Performance records. `public_sources.json` lists the two
monthly source archives, field definitions, and archive checksums.

`substantive_20261028/holdout_boxes/panel_cases/` contains the 94 frozen
evaluation cases, with 16 training service calendars per case. The fields
`n`, `lag`, `delta`, `caps`, `service`, `probabilities`, and `actual_service`
give the aircraft count, travel time in decision intervals, interval length
in minutes, release limits, training service counts, training probabilities,
and the held-out service counts, respectively. Each row of `service` is a
possible sequence of takeoff counts; `actual_service` is used to evaluate
the realized takeoffs. Simulated future reports are generated by the code.

The adjacent `panel_design.json` records case paths and their SHA-256 hashes.
The other directories contain experiment configurations and derived cases
for different release limits. Paths within the frozen manifests are
relative to the repository root. Earlier preprocessing paths and hashes
are retained as provenance; panel evaluation uses the supplied frozen cases.
